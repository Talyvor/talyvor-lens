## Terms for paying people and companies outside Talyvor

These terms are between **TALYVOR LTD**, 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom, WC2H 9JQ
("Talyvor"), and the company or person whose workspace accepts them. A person of the workspace accepts them once,
for the whole workspace, before its first use. They add to Talyvor's terms of service.

1. **What it is.** Your company and its agents may pay people and companies outside Talyvor from their accounts.
2. **Your rules apply.** An agent pays only within the limits, payee lists and approvals you set for it. A payment
   your rules send for approval waits until a person approves it.
3. **Checks first.** Every payee and every payment is screened against sanctions lists before any money moves. A
   payment that matches is refused or held. A person must be verified before they can be paid.
4. **Getting the details right.** You are responsible for the payee's details. A payment sent to the details you
   gave may not be recoverable.
5. **Once it is sent.** A payment that has left the account cannot be cancelled by Talyvor. Talyvor will help you
   ask for it back, but cannot promise it will be returned.
6. **Before real money.** Until Talyvor tells you otherwise, nothing you do with it moves real money, and what it
   holds cannot be paid out or exchanged for real money. Talyvor will tell you, and ask you to
   accept these terms again, before it moves real money.
7. **Your agents.** Your agents use it only within the rules you set, and you are responsible for what they do with
   it, as if you had done it yourself.
8. **Fees.** Any fee is shown before you confirm a use. No fee is charged in real money before it moves
   real money.
9. **Checks and refusals.** Talyvor may refuse, hold or report a use where the law requires it, where a check fails,
   or where an operator has frozen your workspace while a case is reviewed.
10. **New versions.** Talyvor may publish a new version of these terms. You will be asked to accept it, and your
    workspace cannot use this until you do.
11. **Stopping.** You may stop using it at any time. Talyvor may suspend it for your workspace, telling you why
    where the law allows.
