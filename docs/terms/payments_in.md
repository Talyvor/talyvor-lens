## Terms for receiving money from outside Talyvor

These terms are between **TALYVOR LTD**, 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom, WC2H 9JQ
("Talyvor"), and the company or person whose workspace accepts them. A person of the workspace accepts them once,
for the whole workspace, before its first use. They add to Talyvor's terms of service.

1. **What it is.** Your company and its agents may receive money from people and companies outside Talyvor into
   their accounts.
2. **Checks first.** Every payment in is screened against sanctions lists before it is added to an account. A
   payment that matches is refused or held, and Talyvor may have to report it.
3. **Questions about a payment.** Talyvor may ask you where a payment came from and what it is for, and may hold it
   until you answer.
4. **Returning a payment.** A payment that was not meant for you, or that fails a check, may be sent back to the
   payer.
5. **Before real money.** Until Talyvor tells you otherwise, nothing you do with it moves real money, and what it
   holds cannot be paid out or exchanged for real money. Talyvor will tell you, and ask you to
   accept these terms again, before it moves real money.
6. **Your agents.** Your agents use it only within the rules you set, and you are responsible for what they do with
   it, as if you had done it yourself.
7. **Fees.** Any fee is shown before you confirm a use. No fee is charged in real money before it moves
   real money.
8. **Checks and refusals.** Talyvor may refuse, hold or report a use where the law requires it, where a check fails,
   or where an operator has frozen your workspace while a case is reviewed.
9. **New versions.** Talyvor may publish a new version of these terms. You will be asked to accept it, and your
   workspace cannot use this until you do.
10. **Stopping.** You may stop using it at any time. Talyvor may suspend it for your workspace, telling you why
    where the law allows.
